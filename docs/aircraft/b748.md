# Boeing 747-8 Intercontinental (`b748`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -38.5). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Boeing 747-8 Intercontinental (747-8I)
- **Manufacturer:** Boeing
- **Role:** Long-haul wide-body airliner (four-engine)
- **Operator / service:** Commercial
- **First flight:** 2010
- **Crew:** 2 pilots (+ cabin crew)

The "Queen of the Skies": a four-engine, double-deck wide-body with the iconic hump behind the flight deck, which in the 747-8I extends back well past the wing leading edge (about 40 m of continuous upper deck). The wing is swept 37.5° and carries four GEnx engines with chevroned nacelles under it, and the wingtips are raked (no winglets). The tail is a tall fin with a swept horizontal stabiliser; the rear fuselage upsweeps to a sharp tail cone.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Hump: a long, smooth upper deck that begins just behind the cockpit (d ≈ 9) and tapers into the top of the fuselage at d ≈ 40 (the -8I extended upper deck, ~ 5.6 m longer than the -400).
2. Long, sleek fuselage: round main cross-section 6.5 m wide, a "double bubble" under the hump (small step above the cockpit), nose cone pointed and drooped.
3. Four engines under the wing at y = ±11.5 and ±23.5, hung forward of the leading edge on short pylons; nacelle exits have serrated "chevron" edges.
4. Wing: 37.5° sweep, a kinked planform (Yehudi) at ~ 33 % semi-span; raked wingtips (~ 4 m of raking) rather than winglets; large flap-track fairings under the trailing edge (canoe fairings).
5. Body main gear: two 4-wheel bogies under the fuselage plus two 4-wheel bogies under the wings (16 main wheels in total); nose gear with two wheels.
6. Tall fin (19.4 m above the ground) with a two-section rudder; swept horizontal stabilisers (~ 22 m span).
7. Cockpit windows: six (three per side), with the pilots' side windows below the upper-deck hump: flight-deck "eyebrow" windows on the upper deck behind them.
8. Upswept tail cone, with the APU exhaust at the very end.

## 4. Wing planform

Section table (semi-span position y from the centreline; leading-edge distance d from nose; the first row is where the wing meets the fuselage side, and the wing is assumed to continue inside the fuselage at that chord for the area check):

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline unless noted): **560 m²** vs published reference **554 m²** (1.1 %).

- Root chord ~ 14.7 m (fuselage side), kink chord ~ 9.0 m at y = 11.2, tip chord ~ 3.0 m; LE sweep ~ 42° inboard / ~ 36° outboard (quarter-chord ~ 37.5°); TE sweep ~ 14° inboard / ~ 25° outboard; dihedral ~ 6.5° (jig) - the ground-attitude wing gives root at body y ≈ −2.5 and tip at body y ≈ +0.8; incidence ~ +2° root, twist −4° (washout); thickness ~ 12 % root → ~ 8 % tip; airfoil family: advanced supercritical (Boeing 747-8 "new airfoil"). Low wing: the root is at the bottom of the main circle.
- Raked tip: last ~ 4 m of the wing swept at ~ 60° LE, with the tip chord ~ 1.8 m... no winglet. Wing flex in flight: tips rise ~ 3–4 m at 1 g.
- LE devices: Krueger flaps (3 per wing) inboard of the inner engine (y 4–10); variable-camber Krueger/ slats 6 per wing outboard (y 12–32), slat chord ~ 12 %.
- TE: triple-slotted inboard (y 4.5–11) and outboard (y 13–24) flaps, ~ 25 % chord; flaperon/ailerons: inboard "high-speed" aileron y 24–28 and outboard "low-speed" aileron y 28–33, ~ 22 % chord; spoilers: 5 outboard panels (y 13–28) + 2 inboard panels (y 7–12.6) per wing, ~ 12 % chord; used as speedbrakes in flight (all of them) and lift dump on the ground.

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabiliser: span 22.2 m, root chord ~ 8.0 m at the fuselage, tip chord ~ 2.8 m, LE sweep ~ 37.5°, dihedral ~ 7°; mounted on the tail cone at d ≈ 62–70 (root LE d = 61.5), at body y ≈ +1.0; elevator ~ 30 % chord, trimmable stab ±~ 10°.
- Vertical fin: height ~ 10.3 m above the fuselage (tip at body y = +14.2 = 19.4 above ground), root chord ~ 12.5 m (LE root d = 56.5, TE d = 69.0), tip chord ~ 4.2 m, LE sweep ~ 45°; two-section rudder (upper and lower) ~ 30 % chord; swept-back tip.
- No ventral fin (a tail-strike skid under the aft fuselage).

## 6. Propulsion

- Four General Electric GEnx-2B67 high-bypass turbofans (BPR ~ 8): 66,500 lbf (296 kN) each; fan diameter 2.82 m; chevron nozzles.
- Nacelles: length ~ 7.2 m, diameter ~ 3.2 m; inboard centres at y = ±11.5 (hung ~ 2.4 m below the wing chord plane, intake lip ~ 4.7 m ahead of the local LE), outboard y = ±23.5. Intake lip d: inner 30.5, outer 39.3 (outer local LE d ≈ 43.9); nozzle exit d: inner 37.5, outer 46.0.
- Thrust reversers: translating cowl (cascades) modelled on all four engines (the real 747-8 may have reversers on the inboard engines only; unverified).

- Wheelbase (nose to mean main, from table): **30.2 m**; track (outermost contact points): **11 m**.
- Nose gear carries ~8.9 % of weight with the CG at the origin.
- Gear positions measured from the nose tip: nose leg d = 11 m, main legs d = 41.2 m.
- cgHeight (body origin above ground when parked): **5.2 m**; all contact points at y = −cgHeight (level ground).

- Tricycle with body and wing main gear: nose (2 wheels, steers ±70°, retracts forward); 2 wing-gear legs at y = ±5.5 (4-wheel bogies, retract inwards), 2 body-gear legs at y = ±2.5 (4-wheel bogies, retract forwards into the fuselage belly bay), wheel radii: main ~ 0.6 m, nose ~ 0.5 m.
- Static attitude: level (pitch ~ 0° to +1°); belly clearance ~ 2.2 m.
- Gear doors: the wing-gear bay door and body-gear doors (two large pairs); nose gear forward-hinged doors; the body gear is two 4-wheel bogies, one each side of the centreline.
- No hook, no drag chute, auto-brake.

## 8. Cockpit

Pilot eye point (body axes): [-0.55, 1.3, -31.9].

Eye point (captain, left seat): d ≈ 6.6, x = −0.55, body y = +1.3 (~ 6.5 m above the ground). Large upper-deck nose cabin behind the cockpit. Cockpit entrance via the left door.

## 10. Performance

- Max operating speed Mmo 0.90; cruise ~ Mach 0.855 at 10,700 m (35,000 ft); max at sea level ~ 185 m/s (360 kt, Vmo 365 KCAS limit).
- Ceiling 13,100 m (43,000 ft); initial climb ~ 10 m/s at MTOW; time to FL350 ~ 28 min.
- Stall (landing config, 300 t) ~ 68 m/s (~ 132 kt); approach ~ 77 m/s (~ 150 kt VREF+5); rotate ~ 84 m/s (~ 163 kt at 440 t); take-off distance ~ 3,200 m at MTOW (SL ISA); landing ~ 2,100 m at max landing weight.
- Range: ~ 14,815 km (8,000 nm) with ~ 467 passengers.
- G: +2.5 / −1.0 (+2.0 flaps down). Max roll rate ~ 5 °/s. Glide ratio ~ 17:1.
- T/W ~ 0.33 at typical mass (4 × 296 kN); wing loading ~ 650 kg/m².

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Hydromechanical with electronic augmentation (treat as conventional with yaw dampers and electronic stab trim; the 747-8 has partial fly-by-wire for the spoilers and ailerons); auto-throttle, autopilot, autobrake. No envelope protection.
- Flap detents: UP, 1° (take-off/approach with slats), 5°, 10°, 20°, 25°, 30° (landing). Slats extend with flaps ≥ 1 (Krueger inboard). Spoilers: speedbrakes (flight spoilers), ground spoilers (all spoilers) automatically at touchdown.
- Gear: retract time ~ 14 s. Thrust reversers on the ground only (up to ~ 70 % N1).
- Autoland CAT IIIB.
