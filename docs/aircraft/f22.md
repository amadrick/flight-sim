# F-22A Raptor (`f22`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -9.9). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Lockheed Martin / Boeing F-22A Raptor
- **Manufacturer:** Lockheed Martin (with Boeing)
- **Role:** Air-dominance stealth fighter
- **Operator / service:** U.S. Air Force
- **First flight:** 1997
- **Crew:** 1

A large twin-engine, twin-fin stealth fighter with a faceted, diamond-planform wing blended into a broad chined fuselage. Two outward-canted vertical fins sit over widely spaced engine bays, with all-moving horizontal stabilators whose tips line up with the nozzle exit plane. The 2D thrust-vectoring nozzles have rectangular, serrated trailing edges. There are no external stores, no visible weapon pylons, and the surfaces are matte grey.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Diamond wing planform: LE sweep 42°, clipped tips, TE swept forward ~17°; edges are aligned (wing LE parallel to stab LE; wing TE parallel to stab TE and the door edges).
2. Two vertical tails canted ~27° outward, trapezoidal with clipped tops (the highest point is the fin tip).
3. Chined forebody: the nose is a flattened, rounded-diamond cross-section that blends into sharp chines running back to the wing LE.
4. Large bubble canopy with no frame between the windshield and the canopy (one-piece transparency, ~3.7 m long) and a sharply raked, faceted windshield.
5. Intakes: two sharp-lipped, caret (diamond-shaped) side-mounted inlets under the wing LE root, ~6.6 m from the nose; no splitter plate gap — the boundary layer is diverted through a bleed slot, and the lip is swept back.
6. All-moving stabilators mounted far aft, tips just ahead of the nozzles; swept 42°, with trimmed tips.
7. Two rectangular-ish 2D pitch-vectoring nozzles with sawtooth-edged upper and lower flaps, spaced widely apart with a flat tail boom between.
8. Internal weapon bays: one large ventral main bay (between the intakes) and two small side bays at the intake sides; gear doors are sawtooth-edged.
9. Overall: low aspect ratio (2.36), flat bottom, sharp edges everywhere; top of the airframe is a continuous blended surface from nose to the fins.

## 4. Wing planform

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline): **78.9 m²** vs published reference **78.04 m²** (1.1 %).

- Root chord ~8.3 m (at the fuselage side; ~9.4 m extended to the centreline); tip chord ~1.8 m; taper ratio ~0.2.
- LE sweep 42°, TE sweep ~−17° (swept forward), dihedral ~0°, no notable twist (cambered, thickness ~5–6 % t/c; published airfoil is classified; use a thin biconvex-like section with cambered LE).
- Vertical position: mid-low, the wing root is flush with the bottom of the fuselage side chine at body y ≈ −0.2 (just below the FRL).
- Leading-edge flaps (LEF): spanwise ~12 % to 95 % of semi-span, ~15 % chord, scheduled with AoA and Mach.
- Flaperons (trailing edge, inboard of the ailerons): ~15–52 % semi-span, ~25 % chord; ailerons: ~52–90 % semi-span, ~25 % chord. Both can deflect ±25–30°, and flaperons droop for landing.
- No speedbrake on the wing; the speedbrake is a split surface atop the aft fuselage between the fins: ~1.6 m long, ~1.1 m wide, opens to ~55°, at d ≈ 12.4–14.0 m.
- LERX: none in the sense of the F-16; the wing root is a continuous blend with the chine (the chine does that job).

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabilators: all-moving, span 8.84 m overall (tip at y = ±4.42), root LE d ≈ 15.4, root chord ~3.3 m, tip chord ~1.1 m, LE sweep ~42°, ~0° dihedral, trimmed tips, pivot at ~35 % chord. Pitch authority ±~25°; also used differentially for roll.
- Vertical tails: two, canted 27° outward from vertical, root at y = ±1.1 m, base LE d ≈ 13.4, root chord ~4.6 m, tip chord ~1.7 m, LE sweep ~25°, slant height ~3.0 m (tip at 5.08 m above ground), rudders ~30 % of chord, full height, deflect ±30° (rudders also act with thrust vectoring).
- No ventral fins.

## 6. Propulsion

- Two Pratt & Whitney F119-PW-100 afterburning low-bypass turbofans (bypass ratio ~0.3). Dry (military) thrust ~26,000 lbf (~116 kN) each; max (afterburner) ~35,000 lbf class (~156 kN) each. Total ~70,000 lbf+ (the manufacturer's 35,000 lbf class figure).
- Nozzles: 2D convergent–divergent with pitch thrust vectoring ±20° (rate ~ 45°/s); exit rectangular-ish ~1.1 m wide, ~0.75 m tall, centres at x = ±1.15 m, y ≈ 0.1, at the end of the airframe (d = 18.9). Nozzle "radius" in geometry = 0.55 (half-width).
- Intakes: fixed-geometry caret inlets with variable ramp; each ~0.75 m tall × ~0.9 m wide, lip at d ≈ 6.6 m, x = ±1.4 m; diverterless, S-shaped ducts that shield the compressor face.
- Supercruise at ~M1.7–1.8 without afterburner.

- Wheelbase (nose to mean main, from table): **6.1 m**; track (outermost contact points): **4.3 m**.
- Nose gear carries ~9.8 % of weight with the CG at the origin.
- cgHeight (body origin above ground when parked): **1.7 m**; all contact points at y = −cgHeight (level ground).

- Tricycle; nose leg with twin wheels (r ~0.25 m) retracts forward, main legs retract forward and rotate into the intake-side fuselage bays; the nose gear is just aft of the radome at d ≈ 4.4 m (steering ~±60°).
- Static attitude: slightly nose-low to level (deck angle ~0°–1°); fuselage underside ~0.85 m above ground at the centreline.
- Gear doors: saw-tooth edges; main doors open outboard of the bay.
- Arresting hook: stowed under the fuselage between the nozzles, ~d 18.0 m.
- Tires: mains ~ 0.8 m dia (r 0.40 m); nose ~ 0.5 m (r 0.25 m).

## 8. Cockpit

Pilot eye point (body axes): [0, 0.85, -5].

Single-seat bubble canopy hinged at the rear (opens upward and aft); the transparency is ~3.7 m long, ~1.0 m wide. HUD: wide-angle diffractive HUD (30° × 25°). Eye point ~4.9 m behind the nose, ~0.85 m above CG, centred. The ACES II ejection seat is reclined ~20°.

## 9. Masses

- Empty ~19,700 kg (43,430 lb); max take-off 38,000 kg (83,500 lb); typical flying (approx. 60 % fuel + 6 AAM) ~27,000 kg; internal fuel ~8,200 kg (18,000 lb) (~10,200 L at 0.80 kg/L); external tanks 2 × 600 gal (not used in the sim).
- Weapons load for a clean air-to-air sortie: 6 AIM-120 + 2 AIM-9 (~1,500 kg).

Estimated moments of inertia at typical mass (radius-of-gyration method, k = 0.16·span, 0.22·length, 0.24·length; ESTIMATE, derive properly if better data is available): Ixx(roll) ~ 127,000 kg·m², Iyy(pitch) ~ 468,000 kg·m², Izz(yaw) ~ 557,000 kg·m².

Derived at typical mass (27000 kg): wing loading **346 kg/m²**, thrust/weight at max thrust **1.18** (2 × 156 kN).

## 10. Performance

- Max speed: ~Mach 2.25 at altitude (~15,000 m); supercruise ~Mach 1.8 (published > M1.5, reported ~1.82); max speed at sea level ~Mach 1.2 (~410 m/s, ~800 kt).
- Cruise: ~Mach 0.9 (M1.5–1.8 supercruise at 15 km). Service ceiling ~19,800 m (65,000 ft). Initial climb rate ~300 m/s (~60,000 ft/min) at light weight (estimate).
- Stall: not reached (FBW AoA limited, ~60° AoA controllable); equivalent 1-g stall ~60 m/s clean at light weight; landing speed ~72 m/s (approach ~140 kt, AoA ~11°); rotation ~80 m/s (~155 kt); take-off roll ~450 m (1,500 ft) clean; landing roll ~750 m with brakes.
- Range: ~2,960 km ferry with two tanks (~ 1,600 nm combat radius ~ 760 km). Endurance ~2 h internal fuel.
- Turn: sustained ~ 16–18 °/s at M0.9, 15,000 ft (estimate); instantaneous ~ 27–28 °/s near 400 kt (est.). Max roll rate ~ 200–250 °/s (est.).
- G limits: +9.0 / −3.0 (design). Glide ratio ~ 10:1 (est.).
- Thrust-to-weight: ~1.3 at typical mass with afterburner (see §9); with 50 % fuel > 1.1.

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Fly-by-wire (quadruple redundant), integrated flight/propulsion controls. AoA/G limiter (≈ +9 G, ~ 60° AoA allowed in rapid post-stall maneuvers), pitch rate demand with 'G command' behavior, roll rate demand around the velocity vector; sideslip suppression via rudders and differential stab.
- Thrust vectoring: 2D nozzles ±20° in pitch (collective), used for pitch authority at low dynamic pressure; yaw is by rudder (not by vectoring).
- Surfaces: LE flaps, flaperons, ailerons, rudders, stabilators, speedbrake on the upper aft deck (split into 2 panels; opens to 55°).
- Flaps: scheduled; "flaps" = flaperons drooped ~ 25–30° at landing; LE flaps ~ 20–25° down at landing.
- Hook: yes (stowed beneath). No drag chute (sim does not need one).
- Gear: retract time ~ 8–10 s.
- Throttle: the sim maps 0..0.9 idle→MIL, 0.9..1.0 AB. Max supercruise at MIL.

## 12. Markings and finish

- Finish: matte "Have Glass" RAM-coated low-observable grey, overall ~#7d8187 (upper) and ~#6e737a (lower) — essentially one uniform dark-grey tone; panel lines are faint. Canopy: gold-tinted (ITO), colour ~#b79b4a at a glancing angle, reflective.
- National insignia: low-visibility type, dark-grey star-and-bar (~#5a5f66, barely contrasting) on the upper left / lower right wing surfaces and on the fin or aft fuselage sides.
- Tail code: two-letter code on the fins (e.g., "FF"), plus squadron emblem small; serial number on the fins (white-grey, small 30 cm text), "USAF" text on the aft fuselage; no walkway lines.
- Intake danger marks: faint red/black outlined warning stencils near the cheek lips; a red "REMOVE BEFORE FLIGHT" tag is optional.
- Nozzles: darker grey/titanium ~#4a4d52 inside the nozzle area; the sawtooth nozzle flaps are unpainted, ~#6a6e73.

## 13. Confidence notes

- Published: length 18.92 m, span 13.56 m, height 5.08 m, wing area 78.04 m², empty weight, MTOW, fuel capacity, ceilings (~), thrust class. 
- ~Estimates from proportions: all station widths/heights, gear placement, track and wheelbase (~6.1, ~4.3), wing section chords (the wing "root" and "tip" are approximations with the published area as a check), tail dimensions (~), nozzle/intake coordinates, eye point.
- ~ Performance: stall speed, climb rate, turn rates, thrust numbers (dry thrust is a widely quoted figure that is not exact).
- Marking positions are approximate: the real jet's low-visibility markings are barely visible.
