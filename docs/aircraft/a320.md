# Airbus A320neo (`a320`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -17.7). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Airbus A320-251N (A320neo)
- **Manufacturer:** Airbus
- **Role:** Narrow-body short/medium-haul airliner
- **Operator / service:** Commercial
- **First flight:** 2014
- **Crew:** 2 pilots (+ cabin crew)

A single-aisle twin-jet with a rounded, bulbous nose and a distinctive, squared "Airbus-style" cockpit window set (six panes, with the lower front pane and the characteristic "pointed" side-window). The neo version has oversize LEAP-1A/PW1100G engines with sawtooth nacelle exit edges and large wingtip "sharklets" (upturned winglets ~ 2.4 m tall) in place of the traditional A320 simple tips.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Sharklets: upward-curving tips, ~ 2.4 m tall, with the swept leading edge and a canted aspect; a standard feature on the neo (and many ceo).
2. Large high-bypass engines (LEAP-1A fan 1.98 m / PW1100G fan 2.06 m), nacelles with sawtooth (chevron) edges at the exhaust; engine centreline at y = ±5.75 m and mounted well forward of the LE and below the wing.
3. Cockpit windows: six per cockpit including the angular "side-slider" with a characteristic raked bottom edge ("Airbus nose").
4. Single aisle, constant 3.95 m wide fuselage with a rounded top; the fuselage has a pronounced dorsal slope from the nose to the cabin; a pointed upswept tail cone.
5. Fin with a distinctive "drop" at the base (dorsal fin fillet), a swept horizontal stabiliser, with the rudder of ~ 25 % chord and a lightly rounded fin tip.
6. Wing: ~ 25° quarter-chord sweep with trailing-edge flap track fairings (two per wing); wing-body fairing is a short, bulbous belly fairing (the "canoe").
7. Main gear: two-wheel bogies (single axle, dual wheels) retracting inward; nose gear two-wheel, retracts forward.
8. Belly: two cargo doors and the APU exhaust at the end of the tail cone.

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline unless noted): **128.7 m²** vs published reference **122.6 m²** (5 %).

- Root chord ~ 6.3 m (fuselage side), kink chord ~ 3.9 m at y = 6.4, tip chord ~ 1.2 m; LE sweep ~ 28° inboard, 25° outboard (quarter chord 25°); TE ~ 12° → 5° sweep; dihedral ~ 5.1° (root at body y ≈ −1.5, tip at +0.1 on the ground, plus the sharklet to y ≈ +2.3); incidence +~ 2° root, twist ~ −3°; thickness ~ 12.5 % root → ~ 10 % tip; supercritical (Airbus A320 airfoil family). Low wing.
- LE slats: 5 per wing (y 2.6–17), slat chord ~ 11 %, extend 18–27° with the flaps; TE: single-slotted Fowler flaps (inboard y 2.2–6.4, outboard y 7.3–14.0), ~ 24 % chord; aileron y 14.4–17.5 (~ 22 % chord); spoilers: 5 per wing (y 2.8–13.5), used for roll, speedbrake (spoiler 2–5), and lift dump (all).
- Sharklets: a ~ 2.4 m tall wingtip fence blended into the tip, curved ~ 80° from horizontal; chord ~ 1.5 m at the base.

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabiliser: span 12.45 m, root chord ~ 4.0 m (LE root d = 32.0), tip chord ~ 1.2 m, LE sweep ~ 34°, dihedral ~ 5°; elevator ~ 30 % chord; trim ±~ 13°.
- Vertical fin: tip at 11.76 m above ground (~ 6.0 m above the fuselage top), root chord ~ 7.0 m (LE root d = 28.5, TE d = 35.5), tip chord ~ 2.2 m, LE sweep ~ 40°, with a dorsal fillet; rudder ~ 25–30 % chord, single section (±~ 30°). No ventral fin.

## 6. Propulsion

- Two CFM LEAP-1A26 (120.6 kN, 27,120 lbf) — or Pratt & Whitney PW1127G (similar) — geared/direct-drive high-bypass turbofans (BPR 11); fan diameter 1.98 m (LEAP).
- Nacelles: length ~ 4.3 m, diameter ~ 2.4 m, centre y = ±5.75 m, hung ~ 1.5 m below the wing chord; the intake lip at d ≈ 12.8 (3.4 m ahead of the LE), exit d ≈ 17.2; chevron nozzle; exit radius ~ 0.55 m.
- Thrust reversers: translating cowl on both engines. APU in the tail cone.

## 10. Performance

- Mmo 0.82; cruise Mach 0.78 at 11,000 m (TAS ~ 230 m/s); max at sea level ~ 180 m/s (350 KCAS Vmo).
- Ceiling 12,000 m (39,800 ft; 39,100 ft certified); initial climb ~ 14 m/s at MTOW; time to FL350 ~ 21 min.
- Stall (landing config, 65 t) ~ 53 m/s (~ 103 kt); approach ~ 70 m/s (~ 135 kt VAPP at 65 t, ~ 130 at 60 t); rotate ~ 72 m/s (~ 140 kt at 75 t); take-off ~ 2,100 m at MTOW; landing ~ 1,500 m.
- Range ~ 6,500 km (3,500 nm). G +2.5 / −1.0 (+2.0 with flaps). Roll rate ~ 7–10 °/s. Glide ~ 17:1.
- T/W ~ 0.38 at typical (2 × 120.6 kN); wing loading ~ 520 kg/m².

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Fly-by-wire with sidestick: normal law, alternate law, direct law. Protections (normal law): load factor limit +2.5 / −1.0 (flaps clean; +2.0 / 0 with flaps out), pitch attitude limits (+30° / −15°), bank angle limit 67° (spiral stability beyond 33°), high-AoA protection (α-prot, α-floor autotoga, α-max: the sidestick pull will not exceed α-max), high-speed protection (VMO/MMO +~ 6 kt pitch-up demand), and low-energy warning.
- Flap/slat detents: 0, 1 (slats 18°, flaps 0°), 1+F (slats 18°, flaps 10°; take-off), 2 (slats 22°, flaps 15°), 3 (slats 22°, flaps 20°), FULL (slats 27°, flaps 35°). Speedbrake: spoilers 2–5 (up to 40°); ground spoilers at touchdown automatically. 
- Autobrake, autoland CAT IIIB; auto thrust (A/THR) with TOGA/FLX detents. No hook/chute.
