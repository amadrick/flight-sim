# F-35A Lightning II (`f35`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -9.25). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Lockheed Martin F-35A Lightning II (conventional take-off and landing variant)
- **Manufacturer:** Lockheed Martin
- **Role:** Stealth multirole strike fighter
- **Operator / service:** U.S. Air Force (and export partners)
- **First flight:** 2006
- **Crew:** 1

A single-engine stealth fighter that is shorter and stubbier than the F-22, with a broad, deep fuselage, a wide diamond-planform clipped-tip wing, and a pair of outward-canted twin fins. The intakes are bump-type DSI (diverterless supersonic inlets) on the cheeks, the canopy is a large single-piece bubble with a thick bow frame, and the single nozzle is an axisymmetric petal nozzle with sawtooth edges.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Two canted vertical fins (~20° from vertical), clipped tops, mounted well forward on the tail section, with the horizontal stabilators low and aft at the end of the boom.
2. Diverterless supersonic inlets: a fixed bump on the cheeks beside the cockpit and a rectangular-ish scalloped lip with the angled top edge; no splitter plate.
3. Big blended canopy: a high windscreen with a thick metal bow frame, a faceted shape and a rear bubble section; the nose is sharply pointed and droops from the canopy.
4. Wing: 33° LE sweep, 12° forward-swept TE (trapezoid), clipped tips with no tip pylons, with the root flush into the fuselage side.
5. Large flat-bottomed, wide, chined fuselage blended into the wing; the belly is flat, slab-like; side chines run from the nose.
6. Single engine with a tall, round axisymmetric petal nozzle (~1.1 m) at the base of the tail surface; the stabilators extend beyond the nozzle exit plane.
7. Stealth details: saw-tooth edges on all doors and panels; the Electro-Optical Targeting System (EOTS) under the chin as a faceted window (sapphire) below the nose.
8. No external stores in the clean configuration; a refuelling receptacle door on the spine behind the canopy (USAF).

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline unless noted): **41.3 m²** vs published reference **42.7 m²** (-3.4 %).

- Root chord ~5.5 m (at fuselage), tip chord ~1.5 m, taper ~0.27; LE sweep 33°, TE sweep ~−12° to −15°, dihedral ~0° (slight), thickness ratio ~5–6 %; airfoil: thin cambered supercritical (classified).
- Mid-low wing; the root is flush with the lower third of the fuselage at body y ≈ −0.1.
- LE flaps: ~15–95 % semi-span, ~15 % chord, drooping up to ~30° at low speed / high AoA; flaperons: ~8–55 % semi-span, ~28 % chord (trailing); ailerons / outer flaperon zone: ~55–95 % semi-span, ~28 % chord.
- Speedbrake: no dedicated surface; the aft-spine outer panels and split rudders/flaperons open on landing. The F-35A has no airbrake panel; speed reduction by symmetric rudder splitting + flaperon + the sim abstract 'speedbrake'.
- No LERX; the chine blends to the wing.

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabilators: all-moving, span ~6.1 m, root chord ~2.9 m, tip chord ~1.0 m, LE sweep ~35°, ~0° dihedral, nearly rectangular trapezoid; positioned beside the nozzle with pivot near d ≈ 14.2.
- Vertical tails: two, canted 20°, height ~1.9 m from root to tip, root chord ~3.4 m (LE at d≈11.8), tip chord ~1.1 m, LE sweep ~35°, rudders ~35 % chord, full height. Fin tip at 4.38 m above ground.
- No ventral fins.

## 6. Propulsion

- One Pratt & Whitney F135-PW-100: dry 28,000 lbf (~125 kN), afterburner ~43,000 lbf (~191 kN); bypass ratio ~0.57.
- Intakes: twin DSI cheek inlets, ~0.7 m × 0.7 m each, face at d ≈ 7.5 m; the intake lips at ~5.9 m from the nose; ducts S-curve into a single compressor face at d ≈ 8.7 m.
- Nozzle: axisymmetric convergent-divergent with sawtooth petals, exit diameter ~1.0 m, exit at d ≈ 15.0 m, centred. (The F-35B has a swivel nozzle; the A does not.)

- Wheelbase (nose to mean main, from table): **5.6 m**; track (outermost contact points): **3.2 m**.
- Nose gear carries ~8.9 % of weight with the CG at the origin.
- Gear positions measured from the nose tip: nose leg d = 4.15 m, main legs d = 9.75 m.
- cgHeight (body origin above ground when parked): **1.6 m**; all contact points at y = −cgHeight (level ground).

- Tricycle; nose leg (dual wheel, steerable ~±45°) retracts forward into the bay behind the radome (d ≈ 3.5); main legs (single wheel each) retract forward and inward into the fuselage bays alongside the weapon bay.
- Static attitude ~level (deck ~0–2° nose-down); the underside of the fuselage is ~0.8 m above ground.
- Arresting hook: behind the nozzle, below the stabilators (A has hook; for field arrestment).
- Doors: saw-tooth edges; main doors open inward after retraction.

## 8. Cockpit

Pilot eye point (body axes): [0, 0.85, -5.35].

Single-seat bubble canopy with a bow frame (hinged at the rear). The cockpit has a large panoramic touch-screen display; the sim uses a conventional HUD for symbology. Eye point ~3.9 m from the nose, ~0.85 m above CG, centre. Zero-zero Martin-Baker US16E seat.

## 9. Masses

- Empty ~13,290 kg (29,300 lb); max take-off ~31,800 kg (70,000 lb); typical flying ~20,000 kg (about 60 % fuel plus 2 AIM-120 internal); internal fuel 8,278 kg (18,250 lb).
- Weapons in internal bays: 2 × 2,000 lb class bombs and 2 × AIM-120.

Estimated moments of inertia at typical mass (radius-of-gyration method, k = 0.16·span, 0.22·length, 0.24·length; ESTIMATE, derive properly if better data is available): Ixx(roll) ~ 59,000 kg·m², Iyy(pitch) ~ 238,000 kg·m², Izz(yaw) ~ 283,000 kg·m².

Derived at typical mass (20000 kg): wing loading **468 kg/m²**, thrust/weight at max thrust **0.97** (1 × 191 kN).

## 10. Performance

- Max speed: Mach 1.6 (published; limited by structure/heat) at altitude; sea-level maximum ~Mach 1.2 (~400 m/s); max level at 10 km ~ Mach 1.6.
- Cruise ~Mach 0.85–0.9; service ceiling 15,240 m (50,000 ft); initial climb ~230 m/s (~45,000 ft/min est.).
- Stall: not applicable (AoA limiter ~50°); 1-g stall ~ 62 m/s approx; approach ~ 70 m/s (AoA 13°, ~135 kt); rotation ~ 76 m/s (~148 kt); take-off ground roll ~ 520 m at typical weight; landing roll ~ 700 m.
- Range: ~2,200 km combat range on internal fuel (published 1,090 nmi); combat radius ~ 1,090 km (590 nmi).
- Turn: sustained ~ 15–17 °/s (est.), instantaneous ~ 25 °/s; roll ~ 200 °/s. G limit +9.0 / −3.0 (limited to 9 G at AoA).
- T/W ~ 1.0 at typical mass (full AB), wing loading ~ 470 kg/m².

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- FBW with triple redundancy; AoA/G limiters (+9 G; alpha limit ~50°); pitch/roll rate demand, automatic rudder coordination; very well-damped.
- Flaps: LE flaps and TE flaperons deflect together as 'flaps' (landing configuration: LE flaps ~30° down, flaperons ~25° down), the stab provides pitch trim; tail hook: yes.
- Speed brake: none (see §4); spoilers none. Landing: nose-high carefree handling in 'Land' mode (AoA command with aero scheduling).
- Throttle: single engine, 0..0.9 → idle–MIL, 0.9–1.0 → AB.

## 12. Markings and finish

- Finish: ~#8a8d90 overall, dark/matte "Gunship grey" (RAM coating, FS 36118 close to ~#5e6165 at some areas), lighter grey at the underside ~#9ea1a4. Canopy: gold-tinted ~ #a8873c (reflective).
- National insignia: low-visibility, dark-grey (~#555a5e) star-and-bar on the fuselage sides behind the cockpit (not on wings).
- Tail code (two letters) on the fin outboard faces; squadron/serials in small dark text; "U.S. AIR FORCE" in small dark text on the aft fuselage; no walkways; red "DANGER" stencils near the intake lip.
- Panel lines are subtle; saw-tooth edges unpainted.

## 13. Confidence notes

- Published: length, span, height, wing area, empty weight, MTOW, internal fuel, engine thrust class, max Mach, ceiling.
- ~Estimates: wheelbase, track, gear positions, all station widths/heights, wing section chords (tip/root), tail dims, intake face coordinates, cockpit eye point, gyration constants and climb/turn rates.
- Nose-wheel steering angle (~±45°) and flap angles are unverified.
