# B-2A Spirit (`b2`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -9.4). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Northrop Grumman B-2A Spirit
- **Manufacturer:** Northrop Grumman
- **Role:** Strategic stealth bomber (flying wing)
- **Operator / service:** U.S. Air Force
- **First flight:** 1989
- **Crew:** 2 (pilot and mission commander, side by side)

A tailless flying wing with a 33° swept leading edge, a double-W (sawtooth) trailing edge and a gently humped centre body. All control comes from elevons and split drag rudders at the outer trailing edge. The four engines are buried inside the wing with top-mounted, serpentine inlets in a shallow-vee across the nose and exhausts that discharge across the upper surface in recessed troughs just ahead of the trailing edge.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Pure flying wing planform: LE sweep 33° from the nose to the tips; no vertical or horizontal tail; wing tips are sharply pointed with a very small chord.
2. Trailing edge is a "double W": a central beaver-tail point, two inboard notches at about y = ±3.4 m, and two long outboard straight sections swept forward towards the tips.
3. Four rectangular, slightly lipped, jagged-edge engine inlets in a row along the upper surface a little behind the leading edge, with a sawtooth on the front edge of each lip; they sit within a shallow dorsal hump.
4. Pilot windows: two large, angular, triangular-ish panes per side (four main windows total, plus small upper windows), the cockpit hump rises behind the nose with a flat front.
5. Exhaust troughs: four long, narrow slots on the upper surface near the TE, each fed by an engine and with a ridge of saw-tooth edges; the engines are not visible from below.
6. Rounded, blended nose with a very slightly down-drooped centreline "beak"; the fuselage lobe is the thickest part; the wing thins out strongly outboard.
7. Landing gear: two-wheel nose (forward retracting) and a single 4-wheel bogie per main leg, in bays that have sawtooth-edged doors; two bomb bays between the gear.
8. Overall: dark grey semi-matte surface, no visible seams; the underside is pale.

For this flying wing the "fuselage" is the thick centre lobe. Its half-width is the lobe width (where the thickness exceeds ~ 60 % of the maximum); the true planform width at a given station follows the LE sweep (half-width ≈ d / 0.649, up to 26.2 m at the tips) and is given by the wing table. Heights are the local airfoil thickness above and below the FRL.

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline unless noted): **502.2 m²** vs published reference **478 m²** (5.1 %).

- Flying wing: no separate fuselage; LE sweep 33° straight from the nose region (y ≈ 1.8) to the tips; TE: centre beaver-tail point (d = 20.9), notch at y = ±3.4 (d ≈ 17.2), outer point at y ≈ ±10 (d ≈ 18.4), tip at d ≈ 17.6; tip chord ~ 0.6 m (sharp points).
- Thickness: ~ 2.4 m max at the centre body (≈ 11 % of the 20.9 m chord), decreasing to ~ 0.3 m at the tips; airfoil: thick reflexed (self-trimming), flat bottom, upper surface continuous.
- Dihedral ~ 0°–1° (slight), no kink; the wing is at body y ≈ 0 at the centre body, tips at y ≈ +0.1 (flat). Thin tips droop slightly ~ 1°.
- Vertical position: the entire wing; bottom surface flat at body y ≈ −1.0 (centre) with a gradual rise to y ≈ −0.2 at the tip.
- Control surfaces (ELEVONS + SPLIT RUDDERS) — fractions of semi-span (y/26.2): inner elevon pair 0.13–0.38 (beaver tail & inboard notch flaps), middle elevons 0.38–0.64, outer elevons 0.64–0.80; split drag rudders (clam-shell) at 0.80–0.97 (upper and lower panels open together to give drag, differentially for yaw); chord fraction of the elevons ~ 22–28 % of local chord; deflection ±~ 25° (and upwards ~ 30° for the centre).
- Gust Load Alleviation System (GLAS): symmetric flap motion for turbulence suppression.
- No flaps, no slats, no spoilers; speedbrake: both split rudder panels open together (~ 90° upper / 45° lower).

## 5. Tail

- None. B-2: the vertical function is provided by the two outboard split drag rudders (upper/lower clamshell panels at y ≈ 21–25.5, each ~ 4–5 m long, ~ 20 % chord); yaw is generated by drag asymmetry (one side opens). Pitch/roll by the elevons (about four distinct elevon panels per side) and a small centre "beaver tail" trailing section (the beaver tail is a TE extension of the centre body, a fixed fairing at the exhaust ejector area, and moves for pitch trim and for the gust load).

## 6. Propulsion

- Four General Electric F118-GE-100 non-afterburning turbofans (BPR ~ 0.87), each 17,300 lbf (~ 77 kN); total 69,200 lbf (308 kN). Buried in pairs inside the wing; the thrust vector is horizontal.
- Inlets: four fixed, serpentine (S-duct) rectangular inlets on the upper surface at d ≈ 7.2 (lip), each ~ 1.5 m wide × 0.65 m tall, with sawtooth upper lip; the centres at x = ±2.0, ±4.3 (pairs). Compressor faces at d ≈ 10.5 (deep in the wing).
- Exhausts: four long upper-surface trough/ejectors with flattened nozzles ~ 1.1 m × 0.6 m (equivalent nozzleRadius ~ 0.45) at d ≈ 17.4, cooled with mixing air; centres x = ±1.9 and ±4.1. They exit in line with the TE notches so as to shield the infra-red signature.

- Wheelbase (nose to mean main, from table): **6.9 m**; track (outermost contact points): **9.2 m**.
- Nose gear carries ~13 % of weight with the CG at the origin.
- Gear positions measured from the nose tip: nose leg d = 3.4 m, main legs d = 10.3 m.
- cgHeight (body origin above ground when parked): **3 m**; all contact points at y = −cgHeight (level ground).

- Tricycle; nose gear: a twin-wheel strut retracting forward at d ≈ 3.4; main gear: a four-wheel bogie each (two axles with two wheels), at y = ±4.6 m, d ≈ 10.3, retracting forward into the wing bays outboard of the weapons bays; doors sawtooth-edged.
- Static attitude ~ level; the main gear is relatively long (stroke ~ 1 m) because the centre body is low; ground clearance of the belly ~ 1.6 m.
- No tail hook; drag chute: yes (a single, in the rear fairing; used on landing and on rejected take-offs).

## 8. Cockpit

Pilot eye point (body axes): [-0.55, 0.9, -5.5].

Two seats side by side with four main, large, angular windows (two in front, two on the sides), a small rearward overhead window; no HUD in the fighter sense (HUD-free; an avionics "glass cockpit" with 8 flat displays). Pilot eye point (left seat) ~ 3.9 m from the nose at x = −0.55 m, ~ 0.9 m above CG.

## 10. Performance

- Max speed: ~ Mach 0.95 at altitude (~ 12 km); max at sea level ~ 280 m/s (~ Mach 0.82, ~ 540 kt).
- Cruise: Mach 0.85 at ~ 12,000 m (FL400), TAS ~ 250 m/s; service ceiling 15,200 m (50,000 ft); initial climb ~ 15 m/s (3,000 ft/min at MTOW).
- Stall ~ 65 m/s (landing weight ~ 110 t, flaps-less); approach ~ 75 m/s (~ 145 kt at ~ 120 t); rotation ~ 85 m/s (~ 165 kt at ~ 170 t); take-off ~ 2,300 m; landing roll ~ 1,500 m (with chute).
- Range: ~ 11,100 km unrefuelled (~ 6,000 nm) high-altitude; endurance ~ 10+ h (air-refuelling for 30+ h).
- G limits: +2.5 / −1.0 (design). Roll rate ~ 12 °/s. Glide ratio ~ 20:1 (est.; wing L/D ~ 22 at best).
- T/W ~ 0.22 at typical (4 × 77 kN / 140 t); wing loading ~ 293 kg/m².

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Quadruple-redundant fly-by-wire (the flight computer continuously blends elevons, and the Gust Load Alleviation System). It provides pitch/roll commands, speed and altitude hold and an automatic stall/gust protection. The flight control has an automatic AoA limiter (~ 14°); the B-2 is statically unstable in pitch at some CGs and stabilised by the FCS.
- Yaw control: drag from split rudders (differential), generating a yaw moment; also a dedicated "auto rudder coordination" in turns. Pitch trim: elevons + the beaver-tail. Speedbrake: both split rudders open symmetrically.
- Flaps: none (the elevons droop ~ 10° symmetrically at low speeds as "flaps"); gear down ~ 15 s; there are no leading-edge devices. Drag chute: yes; brakes: carbon.
- Auto-trim for the CG shifts with fuel/weapon release.
