# Airbus A380-800 (`a388`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -37.5). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Airbus A380-841/842/861
- **Manufacturer:** Airbus
- **Role:** Ultra-large long-haul double-deck airliner
- **Operator / service:** Commercial
- **First flight:** 2005
- **Crew:** 2 pilots (+ cabin crew)

The world's largest passenger airliner: a full-length double-deck fuselage (an oval, "double bubble" cross-section 7.14 m wide × 8.41 m tall) with four huge engines hung from a broad, 33.5°-swept wing with a 79.75 m span. The nose is blunt and bulbous, the upper-deck cockpit windows sit on a raised brow, the wing-body fairing is long, and the tail is a huge, tall fin (24.1 m above the ground) with a stabiliser on the rear.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Full-length double-deck fuselage: the upper deck runs continuously from the cockpit hump to the tail cone; cross-section is a "double bubble" (a wider lower oval and a narrower upper oval).
2. Four engines (Trent 900 or GP7200) under the wing, at y = ±12.7 and ±25.2 m, with the nacelles ahead of the wing LE on short, thick pylons; the outer engines are placed so that the wingtip gives clearance.
3. Very large wing: 79.75 m span, 845 m² area; the wing is mounted low and has two kinks; the wing root is very thick and its trailing edge is a sweep of nearly 28° with a big inboard flap.
4. Landing gear: nose gear with 2 wheels; a 4-wheel bogie on each wing gear (retracts inward) and two 6-wheel body gears (retract forward); total 22 wheels.
5. Blunt nose with rounded radome and small, raked windscreens; the upper-deck forward fuselage has a smooth blend into the cockpit "hump".
6. Tall tail: fin height ~ 14 m above the fuselage; horizontal stabiliser span 30.4 m; rear fuselage upsweeps to a short tail cone with the APU exhaust.
7. Two-tone belly: the fuselage "belly fairing" extends from d ≈ 26 to d ≈ 50 and merges with the wing.
8. Wingtip fence: a vertical wingtip fence (a small upper/lower fence; ~ 3.5 m tall, 1.7 m deep) rather than a blended winglet.

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline unless noted): **860.8 m²** vs published reference **845 m²** (1.9 %).

- Root chord ~ 17.5 m (fuselage side), kink chord ~ 12.2 m at y = 14.5, tip chord ~ 4.0 m; LE sweep ~ 37° inboard / 33.5° outboard (quarter-chord ~ 33.5°); TE sweep ~ 20° outboard (inboard TE nearly straight); dihedral ~ 5.6° (root at body y ≈ −3.0, tip at +2.1 on the ground); twist ~ −3.5°; thickness ~ 11 % average (thick 15 % at the root), AR 7.53; supercritical airfoil. Low wing; tip raised by flex 4–5 m in flight.
- LE devices: slats: 7 per wing (inboard 2 Krueger-like and outboard 5 slats); slat chord ~ 12 %. TE: inboard flap y 5–14 and outboard flap y 15–30, each ~ 22 % chord, a "drooped" mid-flap, ailerons: inboard aileron (y 30–34) and outboard aileron (y 35–39.5) ~ 20 % chord; spoilers: 8 per wing (y 6–35), used as speedbrakes, roll spoilers and ground spoilers.
- Wingtip fences (one up, one down: ~ 3.5 m tall) at the tip.

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabiliser: span 30.4 m, root chord ~ 11.0 m (LE root d = 59.0), tip chord ~ 3.5 m, LE sweep ~ 38°, dihedral ~ 6°, elevator ~ 28 % chord; trimmable ±~ 10° (trim tank in the stabiliser).
- Vertical fin: tip at 24.09 m above ground (~ 14 m above the fuselage top, ~ 10 % taller than a 747 fin), root chord ~ 17 m (LE root d = 52.0, TE d = 69.0), tip chord ~ 5.5 m, LE sweep ~ 45°, rudder ~ 28 % chord, two-section (upper and lower), ±~ 30°. No ventral fin.

## 6. Propulsion

- Four Rolls-Royce Trent 972-84 (311 kN, ~ 70,000 lbf) or Engine Alliance GP7270 (~ 311 kN), high-bypass turbofans (BPR ~ 8.7); fan diameter 2.95 m (Trent 970) / 2.96 m (GP7200).
- Nacelles: length ~ 6.5 m, diameter ~ 3.9 m; inboard centres y = ±12.7, outboard y = ±25.2, hung ~ 2.5 m below the wing; intake lip d: inboard 28.2, outboard 38.5; nozzle exit d: inboard 35.0, outboard 45.3; exit radius ~ 1.0 m. Only the inboard engines (2 and 3) have reversers (the outer ones do not).
- The outer engines sit further forward of the LE than the inner ones due to wing sweep.

- Wheelbase (nose to mean main, from table): **31.25 m**; track (outermost contact points): **12.5 m**.
- Nose gear carries ~9.1 % of weight with the CG at the origin.
- Gear positions measured from the nose tip: nose leg d = 9.1 m, main legs d = 40.35 m.
- cgHeight (body origin above ground when parked): **5.7 m**; all contact points at y = −cgHeight (level ground).

- Tricycle with wing and body main gears: nose (2 wheels, r ~ 0.55 m, steers ±70°, retracts forward); wing gears at y = ±6.25 m (4-wheel bogies, r ~ 0.6 m, retract inward); body gears at y = ±2.65 m (6-wheel bogies, r ~ 0.6 m, retract forward into the fuselage belly). The body gear is steerable on the real aircraft (the sim treats as fixed).
- Static attitude: level (deck angle 0°), belly clearance ~ 2.2 m; outer engine clearance ~ 1.0 m.
- No hook, chute. Autobrake and brake-to-vacate; the wing-gear steers (hydraulic).

## 8. Cockpit

Two pilots side by side on the upper deck level; six windows (two front panes with a thin centre post, two side-front, two small side). Eye point (captain, left seat): d ≈ 6.0, x = −0.55, body y = +2.4 (~ 7.6 m above the ground; very high).

## 10. Performance

- Mmo 0.89; cruise Mach 0.85 at 10,700 m (TAS ~ 250 m/s); max at sea level ~ 175 m/s (340 KCAS Vmo).
- Ceiling 13,100 m (43,000 ft); initial climb ~ 8 m/s at MTOW; time to FL350 ~ 32 min.
- Stall (landing config, 380 t) ~ 66 m/s (~ 128 kt); approach ~ 76 m/s (~ 147 kt); rotate ~ 83 m/s (~ 161 kt at 560 t); take-off ~ 2,900 m at MTOW (SL ISA); landing ~ 2,100 m.
- Range ~ 15,200 km (8,200 nm) with 525 pax; G +2.5 / −1.0 (+2.0 flaps out); roll rate ~ 5 °/s; glide ~ 17:1.
- T/W ~ 0.28 at typical (4 × 311 kN); wing loading ~ 530 kg/m².

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Digital fly-by-wire with sidestick; Airbus normal/alternate/direct laws as for the A320: load-factor protection (+2.5 / −1.0 clean, +2.0 / 0 flaps out), pitch limit (+30° / −15°), bank limit 67°, α-protection and α-floor, high-speed protection. Roll and pitch with smoothing for the large aircraft; gust load alleviation by spoilers/ailerons; vibration suppression.
- Flap/slat detents: 0, 1 (slats 18°, flaps 0°), 1+F (slats 18°, flaps 8°), 2 (slats 20°, flaps 17°), 3 (slats 20°, flaps 25°), FULL (slats 24°, flaps 32°). Speedbrake: spoilers 3–7; ground spoilers on touchdown.
- Autobrake, auto-thrust, autoland CAT IIIB; no hook/chute.
