# F-15E Strike Eagle (`f15`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -10.2). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** McDonnell Douglas / Boeing F-15E Strike Eagle
- **Manufacturer:** McDonnell Douglas (Boeing)
- **Role:** Dual-role air-to-ground and air-superiority fighter
- **Operator / service:** U.S. Air Force
- **First flight:** 1986
- **Crew:** 2 (pilot + weapon systems officer, tandem)

A big, twin-engine, twin-fin fighter with a high-mounted, clipped-delta-like wing, a fully boxy square-lipped variable-ramp intake on each side of the forward fuselage, a long deep fuselage spine with a hinged speedbrake, and widely spaced vertical tails. The Strike Eagle adds a second crew seat under a long canopy, conformal fuel tanks (CFT) along the fuselage sides and the dorsal-less spine, and usually carries a pylon-heavy load.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Square-cut, wedge-shaped variable-geometry intakes: ~1.05 m wide × 1.2 m tall rectangles with the upper surface hinged; the lips are canted forward at ~12° from the cheek.
2. High shoulder-mounted wing: LE sweep ~38.7°, clipped tips (wingtip LE cut 45° — the tips are blunt), nearly no TE sweep.
3. Two large vertical fins (not canted) set wide apart on the engine nacelles, with squared-off tips; the base chord is large; rudders on the trailing ~30 %.
4. Large all-moving stabilators with a cut-away on the leading-edge inboard and sawtooth tip; tips about at the nozzle plane, mounted low behind the wing.
5. Large rectangular dorsal speedbrake just behind the canopy (~1.2 m × 2.0 m), which opens up and back to ~45°.
6. Tandem two-seat canopy with a long, one-piece bubble, quite large relative to the nose; the nose radome is a rounded ogive.
7. Conformal fuel tanks on the fuselage sides from the wing LE to the engine nozzles (E-model), hardpoints on the CFT edges and under the fuselage.
8. Twin engines with round nozzles that protrude clearly past the tail beams/fins; between the engines a strong "tunnel" with the arresting hook.

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline unless noted): **54.9 m²** vs published reference **56.5 m²** (-2.8 %).

- Root chord ~5.7 m, tip chord ~1.8 m (tip is clipped), taper ~0.32; LE sweep 38.7°, TE sweep ~0° (forward ~1°), dihedral ~ −1° (slight anhedral), thickness ~6.6 % at root to 3 % at tip (NACA 64A006.6 → 64A203).
- Shoulder/high wing: root at body y ≈ +0.5 m (mid-fuselage depth, ~0.25 above the FRL on the 'upper' side).
- Flaps: ~12–48 % semi-span, ~27 % chord (plain); ailerons ~48–88 % semi-span, ~27 % chord; no LE flaps (fixed conical camber); there are no spoilers (roll is aileron + differential stab). Wing tip: LE cut ~45°.
- Speedbrake: single large dorsal panel behind the canopy, hinged at the rear edge, ~2.0 m long; opens to ~45–67°.

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabilators: all-moving, span ~8.6 m (tips at ±4.3), root chord ~3.4 m, tip chord ~1.4 m, LE sweep ~45°, anhedral ~ −3°; located at the nozzle plane, pivot d≈ 17.8, inboard edge cut for the nacelles.
- Vertical tails: two, no cant (vertical), root chord ~4.5 m, tip chord ~1.8 m, LE sweep ~36°, height ~2.8 m above the fuselage; the fin tip is at 5.63 m above ground; fins at y = ±1.55; rudders ~ 30 % chord, full height, ±25°.
- No ventral fins (the E has under-fuselage hardpoints). The tail beams each contain a flare dispenser or boom.

## 6. Propulsion

- Two Pratt & Whitney F100-PW-229 (IPE) afterburning turbofans (E: also -220 or GE F110-GE-129 in later blocks): dry ~17,800 lbf (79.2 kN), max AB ~29,000 lbf (129 kN) each; BPR 0.36.
- Intakes: rectangular variable-geometry with three-ramp ducts; each intake ~1.0 m wide × 1.1 m tall; lip at d ≈ 6.3 m; engine face at d ≈ 11.5 m (x = ±0.95, y = +0.1).
- Nozzles: axisymmetric convergent–divergent with ~28-flap petals, exit diameter ~1.1 m at d ≈ 19.2; engine centres at x = ±1.0 m, y ≈ 0.2 (pairs separated by an ~0.9 m tunnel).

- Tricycle; nose gear (twin wheels) steers (±~45°) and retracts forward into the bay at d ≈ 5.3; main gear single-wheel legs retract forward into fuselage-bay bellies, rotating 90°, main doors in the lower fuselage; tires ~0.9 m diameter.
- Narrow track (~2.75 m); static attitude ~level with a slight nose-high (~1°).
- Hook: aft of the engines on the underside between nozzle beams.

## 8. Cockpit

Pilot eye point (body axes): [0, 1.05, -5.4].

Tandem cockpit in a single long bubble canopy (frame-less, front arch plus two slim bows), hinged at the rear, ~3 m long. Eye point of the pilot ~4.8 m aft of the nose, ~1.05 m above CG, centred; the WSO sits ~0.9 m behind with a raised floor. Wide-angle HUD.

## 9. Masses

- Empty ~14,300 kg (31,700 lb); max take-off 36,700 kg (81,000 lb); typical ~25,000 kg (full internal fuel + 4 AAM + CFT fuel partly); internal fuel ~6,100 kg (13,455 lb); CFT adds ~3,900 kg; 3 × external tanks +~ 5,000 kg.

Estimated moments of inertia at typical mass (radius-of-gyration method, k = 0.17·span, 0.22·length, 0.24·length; ESTIMATE, derive properly if better data is available): Ixx(roll) ~ 123,000 kg·m², Iyy(pitch) ~ 457,000 kg·m², Izz(yaw) ~ 544,000 kg·m².

Derived at typical mass (25000 kg): wing loading **442 kg/m²**, thrust/weight at max thrust **1.05** (2 × 129 kN).

## 10. Performance

- Max speed: Mach 2.5 at altitude (clean, short bursts; ~Mach 2.3 combat-configured); max at sea level ~ Mach 1.2 (~ 400 m/s).
- Cruise: ~Mach 0.9. Service ceiling 18,300 m (60,000 ft); initial climb ~ 250 m/s (50,000 ft/min).
- Approach ~ 75 m/s (145 kt), stall ~ 65 m/s (clean; ~60 m/s in landing configuration), rotation ~ 80 m/s; take-off roll ~ 760 m; landing roll ~ 850 m.
- Range ~ 3,900 km ferry (CFT + 3 tanks); combat radius ~ 1,270 km.
- Turn: sustained ~ 15 °/s, instantaneous ~ 22 °/s; roll rate ~ 230 °/s. G +9.0 / −3.0.
- T/W ~ 1.0 at typical weight (AB); wing loading ~ 440 kg/m².

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Conventional hydromechanical control with a stability-augmentation (CAS) system: pitch/roll/yaw dampers; no FBW envelope limiter but an AoA limiter (AoA limiting above ~29° via stick-shaker/limiter).
- Flaps: 'Landing' ~ 30° down; take-off setting ~ 15°; no LE devices. Speedbrake: dorsal ~ 45°. Hook: yes. No drag chute.
- Roll: ailerons + differential stab; yaw: rudders (twin).

## 12. Markings and finish

- Finish: "Gunship Gray" two-tone upper (~#8d9298 FS 36118), lighter underside (~#a7acb0) — standard post-90s: overall gloss grey ~#868b91.
- National insignia: low-vis star/bar (~#575b60) on the fuselage sides behind the intakes and wings; tail code (2 letters) on the fins with a coloured fin stripe: (use a fictional squadron colour such as ~#c7b02a); "U.S. AIR FORCE" in black on the aft fuselage; serial in white/black on the fin.
- Intake: red/white "DANGER" stencils on the intake lip; refuelling door arrow on the spine; walkway markings (thin black) at the wing roots.
- CFT: same grey; weapon pylons small grey.
