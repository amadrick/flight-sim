# F/A-18E Super Hornet (`fa18`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -9). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Boeing F/A-18E Super Hornet (single-seat; F/A-18F two-seat sister)
- **Manufacturer:** Boeing (McDonnell Douglas)
- **Role:** Carrier-based multirole strike fighter
- **Operator / service:** U.S. Navy
- **First flight:** 1995
- **Crew:** 1

A large twin-engine, twin-fin carrier fighter that grew from the F/A-18 Hornet: about 25 % bigger, with big, trapezoidal (rectangular) intakes, enlarged leading-edge extensions and a distinctly bulged mid-fuselage. The twin fins are canted 20° outward and are tall and square-tipped. The wings are mid-set, straight-ish, with wingtip AIM-9 rails and a full-span folding hinge.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Rectangular, canted intakes with sharp, raked top lips under the LERX; the lips are squared-off boxes — the signature difference from the oval Hornet intakes.
2. Large LERX (leading-edge extensions) starting at the cockpit sill and running to the wing root, with a visible dogtooth at the LE.
3. Twin vertical tails canted 20° outward, with large rudders; fin tips are square-cut with a notch; the tail has high tail-boom rear.
4. Mid/high-mounted wing with LE sweep ~20°, a forward-swept TE inboard at the flaps, and drooped ailerons; AIM-9 rails at the wingtips; folding wing pivot near mid-span.
5. Blown, bubble canopy with a single arched frame behind the pilot, slightly raised spine behind it (the back of the "turtle deck") filled with avionics.
6. Faceted, droop-snoot radome with a pointed profile; the nose has chined cross-section.
7. The tail hook hangs below the rear fuselage between the nozzles; the twin nozzles are spaced ~1.5 m apart, with the stab all-moving, tips nearly in line with the nozzles.
8. Gear: long stroke, wide, single nose wheel pair with a catapult launch bar visible, and the shoulder-high wing: large flaps + ailerons droop together when landing.

## 4. Wing planform

Section table (semi-span position y from the centreline; leading-edge distance d from nose; the first row is where the wing meets the fuselage side, and the wing is assumed to continue inside the fuselage at that chord for the area check):

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline unless noted, plus 1.5 m² of extra surface): **46.6 m²** vs published reference **46.5 m²** (0.1 %).

- Wing root chord ~4.7 m (fuselage), tip chord ~2.0 m; LE sweep ~20°, TE sweep ~−3° (nearly straight, slightly forward), dihedral ~ +2° (small); thickness ~5 % (NACA 65A005 mod.). The published area also includes ~1.5 m² of LERX beyond the table (the table stops at the AIM-9 rail root; rail adds ~0.6 m to the span).
- Mid-high wing: root at body y ≈ 0.0 to +0.1 (about at the cockpit-sill level).
- LERX: from d ≈ 3.2 (start) to the wing root at ~8.0, max span ~3.0 m, LE sweep ~70°.
- LE flaps: ~10–95 % semi-span, ~15 % chord; scheduled with AoA/Mach: down to ~30°.
- TE flaps: ~10–50 % semi-span, ~30 % chord (plain, double-hinged slotted, with 'half' = 30°, 'full' = 45°, drooped); ailerons: ~55–92 % semi-span, ~28 % chord (also droop 45° with flaps — "flaperon" role in landing).
- Wing fold: hinge near y = 3.5 m (about 55 % semi-span), outer panels fold up (carrier handling).
- Speedbrake: the sim uses a split-flap/rudder splay and spoilers; real: speed brake via flaperon+rudder splay (a dorsal brake panel on top of the aft spine, just ahead of the fins, ~1.1 m × 0.8 m).

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabilators: all-moving, span ~8.6 m (tip at y = ±4.3 m), root chord ~3.4 m, tip chord ~1.2 m, LE sweep ~35°, dihedral ~ −2° (slightly anhedral); pivot at d ≈ 15.8.
- Vertical tails: two, each canted 20° outward, root chord ~3.7 m (LE ~12.5 from nose), tip chord ~1.3 m, LE sweep ~35°, vertical height ~2.6 m, rudders ~30 % chord, full height; fin tip ~4.88 m above ground.
- No ventral fins.

## 6. Propulsion

- Two GE F414-GE-400 afterburning low-bypass turbofans (BPR 0.25): dry ~14,000 lbf (62 kN); afterburner ~22,000 lbf (97.9 kN) each; ~44,000 lbf total.
- Intakes: boxy, fixed-geometry, rectangular, canted (hinged slant at the top lip), ~0.9 m wide × ~1.0 m tall, lip at d ≈ 5.7 m, faces at x = ±1.1, y = −0.2; ducts converge to the engine faces.
- Nozzles: axisymmetric convergent–divergent petals (the F414 has a 'divergent flap' with sawtooth in newer builds), exit diameter ~1.0 m, centres at x = ±0.8, y ≈ 0.0, at d ≈ 18.0.

- Wheelbase (nose to mean main, from table): **5.4 m**; track (outermost contact points): **3.4 m**.
- Nose gear carries ~10.2 % of weight with the CG at the origin.
- cgHeight (body origin above ground when parked): **1.55 m**; all contact points at y = −cgHeight (level ground).

- Tricycle: nose gear is a dual-wheel unit, forward-retracting, with a launch bar; main gear single-wheel units retract forward-inward into the fuselage bays. 
- Static attitude ~1–2° nose-down; long-stroke carrier gear is stiff.
- Tail hook: behind the nozzles under the fuselage (d ≈ 17.5).

## 8. Cockpit

Pilot eye point (body axes): [0, 0.85, -4.7].

Single-seat bubble canopy (a frame-less blown bubble with a slim single rear arch); canopy hinges at the rear and opens to the side-up on a pivot; HUD at the top; eye point ~4.4 m from the nose: ~0.85 m above CG, centred, ejection seat Martin-Baker NACES.

## 9. Masses

- Empty ~14,552 kg (32,081 lb); max take-off 29,900 kg (66,000 lb); typical flying ~21,000 kg; internal fuel ~6,530 kg (14,400 lb) (external 3 × 480 gal tanks: +~4,500 kg).
- Max carrier landing weight ~ 20,400 kg.

Estimated moments of inertia at typical mass (radius-of-gyration method, k = 0.16·span, 0.22·length, 0.24·length; ESTIMATE, derive properly if better data is available): Ixx(roll) ~ 100,000 kg·m², Iyy(pitch) ~ 341,000 kg·m², Izz(yaw) ~ 406,000 kg·m².

Derived at typical mass (21000 kg): wing loading **452 kg/m²**, thrust/weight at max thrust **0.95** (2 × 97.9 kN).

## 10. Performance

- Max speed: Mach 1.8 (published ~) at altitude; sea-level max ~Mach 1.2 (clean).
- Cruise ~Mach 0.85; service ceiling 15,000 m (49,000 ft); climb ~ 230 m/s.
- Approach speed ~ 70 m/s (137 kt at carrier weight, AoA 8.1°), stall ~ 60 m/s (clean), rotation ~ 75 m/s; take-off roll ~ 400–500 m (catapult launch assist ~ 3 s to 150 kt).
- Range ~ 2,300 km ferry on internal+external fuel; combat radius ~ 720 km.
- Turn: sustained ~ 18 °/s, instantaneous ~ 28 °/s; roll rate ~ 200 °/s. G +7.5 / −3.0 (airframe; aircraft limiter +7.5 g).
- T/W ~ 1.0 at typical weight with AB; wing loading ~ 450 kg/m².

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Digital FBW (command augmentation): AoA limiting ~ 40° and stable control to high AoA; roll control through ailerons, differential stab, rudders (toe-in at high AoA).
- Flaps (carrier): AUTO, HALF, FULL (flaps ~ 30° half, ~ 45° full) with LE flaps scheduled; hook down for arrestment; catapult hold-back bar and launch.
- Speedbrake: split flap/rudder plus the dorsal panel; for the sim treat 'speedbrake' as the dorsal one. Gear: retracts in ~8 s.
- Wing fold is not animated in flight.

## 12. Markings and finish

- Finish: USN two-tone grey: upper surfaces "Gunship" ~#8e949b (FS 36375 Light Ghost grey) with a darker panel around the intakes, undersides lighter grey ~#a9aeb3 (modern low-vis jets are almost uniform ~#7d8186). Canopy tint clear/gold ~#9d8648.
- National insignia low-visibility (dark grey star-bar ~#58595b) on the fuselage sides and wings; Tail code (two letters + number "AB 300"…) in dark grey on the fins; "NAVY" on fuselage side in black (~#202225).
- Intake danger markings: red/white hazard stripes at intake lips; tail hook in black/yellow; nose cone dark grey; walkway markings (thin black lines) on the wing roots; ejection-seat triangle markings on the canopy sill.
- A catapult launch bar zone: yellow/white markings on the nose gear leg.
