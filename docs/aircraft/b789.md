# Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner (`b789`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -29.9). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner
- **Manufacturer:** Boeing
- **Role:** Long-haul wide-body airliner (twin-engine, composite)
- **Operator / service:** Commercial
- **First flight:** 2013
- **Crew:** 2 pilots (+ cabin crew)

A sleek, twin-engine composite wide-body with a smooth, gently tapered nose, large dimmable windows (tall rounded rectangles), and a very flexible, high-aspect-ratio wing with raked tips. Engines are slung forward under the wing with nacelle exits cut with sawtooth "chevrons". The fuselage is a smooth rounded oval with no panel-joint lines.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Sawtooth (chevron) edges on the trailing lip of the engine nacelles (and on the engine core nozzle).
2. Raked wingtips (~ 2 m of rake), the wing flexes upward in flight; wing LE sweep 32.2° (quarter chord).
3. Large window openings: ~ 0.28 m × 0.47 m tall rounded rectangles, ~ 0.5 m pitch, ~ 1.5 m above the floor; the cockpit windows are four, angular, "cat-eye" shaped with a swept-back shape.
4. Smooth, nearly oval (5.77 m wide × 5.97 m tall) fuselage, no visible rivets or panel seams; a gently sloped nose with a short, dropped radome.
5. Raised, wide-chord tail; the horizontal stabiliser has ~ 19 m span, the fin is relatively tall with a swept tip, and the tail cone is upswept and blunt.
6. Engines (GEnx-1B or Trent 1000): large nacelles hung forward of the wing at y = ±9.5 m; the engines are slightly nose-down relative to the wing.
7. Wing-body fairing: a smooth, long blended fairing that covers the wing roots from d ≈ 20 to 40; no visible flap-track fairings except small canoe pods.
8. Main landing gear: 6-wheel bogies; the nose gear is two-wheel.

- Root chord 10.6 m, kink chord 6.8 m (y = 11), tip chord 2.4 m; LE sweep ~ 35° inboard / ~ 32° outboard (quarter-chord 32.2°); TE sweep ~ 8° inboard / ~ 19° outboard; dihedral ~ 5.5° on the ground (jig lower, flexes up 3–4 m in flight): root at body y ≈ −1.9, tip at body y ≈ +0.7; twist ~ −4° washout; thickness ~ 12 % root → 8 % tip (supercritical), AR ~ 9.6. All-composite.
- Low wing; raked tip last ~ 2 m with a tip chord of ~ 1.3 m.
- LE slats: 7 per wing (variable-camber Krueger flaps in the inboard first, three), slat chord ~ 12 %; TE: inboard flap (y 3.0–11) and outboard flap (y 12–22), single-slotted, ~ 24 % chord, 'drooped hinge' flaperon (y 22–25.5) and aileron (y 26–29.5, ~ 22 % chord); spoilers 7 per wing (y 4–29), 12 % chord. The TE devices droop together ("camber control") in cruise.

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabiliser: span ~ 19.2 m, root chord ~ 6.2 m (LE root d = 50.8), tip chord ~ 1.9 m, LE sweep ~ 35°, dihedral ~ 6°; elevator ~ 30 % chord; trim ±~ 10°.
- Vertical fin: tip at 17.0 m above ground (~ 11.8 m above the fuselage top), root chord ~ 9.5 m (LE d = 45.5, TE d = 55.0), tip chord ~ 3.3 m, LE sweep ~ 43°; rudder ~ 28 % chord, single section (±~ 25°). No ventral fin; tail skid.

## 6. Propulsion

- Two General Electric GEnx-1B74/75 (or Rolls-Royce Trent 1000-TEN) turbofans: ~ 74,100 lbf (330 kN) each; fan 2.82 m; BPR ~ 9.
- Nacelles: length ~ 5.6 m (GEnx), diameter ~ 3.4 m, centres at y = ±9.5 m, ~ 2.1 m below the wing chord plane, intake lip at d ≈ 21.3 (about 5 m ahead of the wing LE at that station), exit d ≈ 28.2; chevron nozzles; exit radius ~ 0.85 m.
- Electrical (no bleed-air) systems: no engine bleed to cabin; the nacelle thrust reversers: translating cowl, both engines.

## 8. Cockpit

Pilot eye point (body axes): [-0.5, 0.9, -24.9].

Two pilots side by side; four large cockpit windows (two front, two side-front) plus two small sliding windows; the front panes are angular "cat-eye" with a swept shape. Eye point (captain, left seat): d ≈ 5.0, x = −0.5, body y = +0.9 (~ 5.1 m above the ground).

## 10. Performance

- Mmo 0.90; cruise Mach 0.85 at 11,000 m (TAS ~ 250 m/s); max at sea level ~ 170 m/s (330 KCAS Vmo).
- Ceiling 13,100 m (43,000 ft); initial climb ~ 12 m/s at MTOW; time to FL350 ~ 22 min.
- Stall (landing, 180 t) ~ 59 m/s (~ 115 kt); approach ~ 70 m/s (~ 136 kt); rotate ~ 77 m/s (~ 150 kt at 240 t); take-off ~ 2,900 m at MTOW (SL ISA, ~ 2,000 m lighter); landing ~ 1,500 m.
- Range ~ 13,950 km (7,530 nm); G +2.5 / −1.0; roll rate ~ 6 °/s; glide ~ 19:1.
- T/W ~ 0.34 at typical (2 × 330 kN); wing loading ~ 530 kg/m².

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Digital fly-by-wire with Boeing-style envelope protection (soft limits: pilots can override with firm inputs): bank angle protection, overspeed and stall protection (stall: stick shaker, then a pitch-down force at the limit AoA), auto-thrust; gust suppression (flight-control law raises the wing flex loads).
- Flap detents: UP, 1, 5, 10 (rare), 15, 17, 18, 20, 25, 30 (most common landing: 30, with 25 for the shorter field). Slats follow the flaps. Spoilers: speedbrake lever; auto speedbrake at landing. 
- Electric brakes, electro-hydraulic gear, autoland CAT IIIB, no hook/chute.
