# F-14D Tomcat (`f14`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -11.5). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Grumman F-14D Super Tomcat
- **Manufacturer:** Grumman
- **Role:** Carrier-based variable-sweep fleet-defence fighter
- **Operator / service:** U.S. Navy (retired 2006)
- **First flight:** 1970
- **Crew:** 2 (pilot + radar intercept officer, tandem)

A big twin-engine carrier fighter with variable-sweep wings (20° to 68°, plus 75° "oversweep" for deck parking), twin widely spaced vertical fins, and a flat, wide "pancake" lifting body between two engine nacelles. The nose is long and pointed, with a large radar dish inside, and a bubble tandem canopy sits above wide, rectangular box intakes.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Variable-sweep wing: at 20° span 19.55 m; at 68° span 11.65 m; the wing pivots at ±3.2 m from the centreline, with fixed glove panels (a swept strake) inboard of the pivot.
2. Wide flat "pancake" centre-section between the nacelles, a tunnel under the fuselage carrying the Phoenix/Sparrow missiles semi-recessed.
3. Two vertical tails spaced wide apart (~3.8 m), tall, slightly rounded-tip trapezoids with dorsal-fin extensions; tail ends flush with nacelle tailbeams; a central stinger housing the hook and the radar-warning blade.
4. Large rectangular, variable-ramp box intakes under the glove, with sharp lips, ahead of the wing pivot.
5. Long, slender, pointed nose (radome ~2.9 m) with a bullet-shaped tip, and small chin pod under the nose (IRST on D model).
6. Tandem bubble canopy with a narrow frame between the pilot and RIO sections; the canopy is long, with high-set seats.
7. All-moving stabilators with swept tips, sitting outboard of the nozzles on tail booms; two aerodynamic dorsal speedbrakes (upper and lower, on the aft between the engines).
8. Widely spaced twin nozzles with a large gap between them, which holds the tail-hook stinger.

Planform area from the table (both sides, root chord carried to the centreline unless noted, plus 14.4 m² of extra surface): **52.5 m²** vs published reference **52.5 m²** (0.1 %).

- Movable panel: pivot at y = ±3.2 m (LE d ≈ 10.5 at the pivot), panel length 7.0 m, root chord ~4.3 m, tip chord ~1.5 m, LE sweep variable 20°–68° (+ 75° oversweep for parking). The fixed glove inboard of the pivot has LE sweep ~ 70° (blended with the fuselage), root-to-pivot.
- Sweep schedule: automatic (Mach/altitude): 20° for take-off/landing/low speed; 20°–35° for combat manoeuvring (M0.7–1.0); ~ 45°–68° at M1.2+; 68° above M ~ 1.4. Manual override is available; 75° oversweep is used only on the ground.
- Dihedral ~0° (straight, level); airfoil NACA 64A209.65 mod.: thickness ~ 9–10 % at root; the wing is high shoulder-mounted at body y ≈ +0.35.
- High-lift: LE slats full span of the movable panel (~10–95 %), extended ~ 17° (automatic with sweep/AoA); TE double-slotted flaps ~ 10–85 % panel span, ~ 30 % chord, to ~ 35° (hydraulic), and a 'maneuver flap' setting ~ 10° at combat.
- Roll: spoilers (4 per wing, inboard panels, 'flight spoilers' ~ 40 % semi-span width) plus differential stabilators; no outboard ailerons.
- Glove vanes: small extendable canard-like vanes in the glove (not on the D; deactivated).

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabilators: all-moving, span ~9.9 m (tips y = ±4.95; with the wing at 20°, tip of the wing at ±9.775), root chord ~3.4 m, tip chord ~1.4 m, LE sweep ~ 47°, ~ 0° dihedral, pivots at d ≈ 17.5, on booms outboard of the nozzles (y from ±1.45 to 4.95).
- Vertical tails: two, each root chord ~4.5 m, tip chord ~1.7 m, LE sweep ~ 40°, height ~ 2.6 m, slight outward lean ~ 3°; fin centres at y = ±1.9; rudders ~ 35 % chord, ±30°. Fin tips at 4.88 m above ground.
- Ventral: two small ventral fins under the tail beams (~0.5 m tall, canted 15°).

## 6. Propulsion

- Two General Electric F110-GE-400 afterburning low-bypass turbofans (D model): dry ~16,600 lbf (73.9 kN), AB ~ 27,600 lbf (122.8 kN) each; BPR 0.76. (A/B models had TF30-P-414A with 20,900 lbf AB with 12,350 dry.)
- Intakes: two-dimensional rectangular variable-ramp intakes ~1.05 m wide × 1.3 m tall; lip at d ≈ 7.6; ducts are slanted; centre x = ±1.5; engine face at d ≈ 12.5.
- Nozzles: axisymmetric convergent–divergent petals, exit diameter ~1.1 m, centre x = ±1.05, y = +0.1 at d ≈ 19.0; 1.0 m between nozzles and centred stinger/hook.

- Static attitude ~ 1–2° nose-low (deck angle ~0°).

## 10. Performance

- Max Mach ~2.34 at altitude (wings 68°; ~ 2.4 for a clean A model); max sea-level speed ~ Mach 1.2 (~ 410 m/s).
- Service ceiling ~ 16,150 m (53,000 ft); initial climb ~ 230 m/s (45,000 ft/min); cruise ~ Mach 0.85.
- Approach ~ 70 m/s (~ 135 kt, AoA ~ 15°, 20° sweep), stall ~ 59 m/s (landing config, wings 20°); catapult/rotate ~ 78 m/s (~ 150 kt); take-off ~ 500 m.
- Range ~ 2,900 km ferry; combat radius ~ 700 km. Turn: sustained ~ 14 °/s (wings 35°), instantaneous ~ 21 °/s; roll rate ~ 180 °/s; G +6.5 / −2.0 (D: +7.5). 
- T/W ~ 0.9–1.0 at typical (AB); wing loading ~ 505 kg/m² at 20°.

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Control: hydraulic with analogue/digital stability augmentation (CAS); yaw: rudders; pitch: stabs; roll: spoilers (below 57° sweep) + differential stabs; direct-lift control (DLC) at landing via spoilers.
- Variable sweep: automatic schedule keeps sweep as function of Mach (see §4), 20°–68°; manual positions 20°/68°/75°(parking). Wing sweep rate ~ 7°/s (full travel ~ 7 s). The slats (up to 17°) and the flaps (up to 35°) lock out with sweep >~ 20°; with the aircraft swept > 57° the spoilers lock out.
- Speedbrake: upper and lower panels (aft fuselage between the engines), open to ~ 60°; arrestor hook; no drag chute.
- Flap detents: UP; MANEUVER (flaps 10°, slats 7°); FULL (flaps 35°, slats 17°, DLC).

## 12. Markings and finish

- Finish: late-war "Compass Ghost / Ghost Grey" two-tone ~ #9aa0a6 upper and ~ #bdc2c6 lower, or a darker low-visibility overall grey ~ #6f757b. The D model carried FS 36375/36320 tones; the nose radome is a lighter grey ~#b6baba.
- National insignia (low-vis: dark grey ~#555a5f) on the fuselage sides aft of the cockpit and on the outer upper wing; tail codes (e.g., "AC 100" as fictional) on the fins in black/dark; "NAVY" on the fuselage side in black; fictional squadron fin colours.
- Intake: red/white "DANGER" outlines; glove edges have black walkway lines; the engine intakes' inner walls dark grey; the nozzle area is bare titanium/dark-metal ~ #4c4f52.
