# A-10C Thunderbolt II (`a10`) — modelling and flight-model data sheet

Body axes: origin at nominal CG, +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose, metres. "d" = distance aft of the nose tip. Body z = d + noseZ (noseZ = -7.35). Values marked "~" are estimates; see Confidence notes.

## 1. Identity

- **Designation:** Fairchild Republic A-10C Thunderbolt II ("Warthog")
- **Manufacturer:** Fairchild Republic (upgrade by Lockheed Martin / Boeing)
- **Role:** Close air support / ground attack
- **Operator / service:** U.S. Air Force
- **First flight:** 1972
- **Crew:** 1

A straight-winged, twin-engine, twin-tail ground-attack aircraft built around the GAU-8 30 mm cannon. Two high-bypass turbofans are mounted high on the rear fuselage, on pylons, in front of the twin vertical stabilisers at the ends of the horizontal tailplane. The wing is large, broad and thick, with drooped tips, and the nose is a blunt, short fuselage with a big bubble canopy.

**Must-get-right features:**
1. Large straight wing, ~17.5 m span, low-set, with a thick root (~16 % t/c); the inner panels are flat with no dihedral, the outer panels have ~7° dihedral and drooped wingtips.
2. Two TF34 turbofan nacelles mounted high on the rear fuselage with ~1.4 m centre offset, ahead of the tailplane; nozzle exit is forward of the tail; pylon-like stub fairings.
3. Twin vertical stabilisers at the tips of the straight horizontal tail (~5.5 m span), each with a rudder; fins are lightly rounded rectangles with slight rake.
4. Bubble canopy, frameless, mounted high above the nose and forward, giving all-round vision; the pilot sits in a titanium "bathtub".
5. GAU-8/A seven-barrelled cannon occupies the whole nose, slightly offset to port so the firing barrel is on the centreline, so the nose gear is offset ~0.3 m to starboard (right).
6. Fixed, partly exposed main gear: the wheels retract rearward into the nacelles/wing roots (partially exposed after retraction); large blister fairings.
7. Eleven weapon hardpoints under the wing and fuselage; pylon outlines; ECM pods at the wingtips (in some configurations).
8. Split ailerons (outer, double hinged) act as speedbrakes; the flaps are slotted, wide-chord, inboard.

- Inboard section (y 0.8–4.0) rectangular, chord ~3.1–3.2 m; outboard (y 4.0–8.765) tapered, chord ~3.1 → 1.6 m; LE sweep ~ 0° inboard, ~ 6° outboard (slight), TE swept ~ −12° (forward) outboard; dihedral: 0° inboard, ~ +7° outboard; thickness ~ 16 % root, ~ 12 % tip (NACA 6716 → 6713); low wing: root at body y ≈ −0.5 (flush with the lower fuselage).
- Wing tips drooped/bent down ~ 20° in the outermost ~ 0.5 m ("drooped tips").
- LE: fixed (cambered), no slats; TE: slotted flaps ~ 10–50 % semi-span, 30 % chord, to 28°; ailerons ~ 50–95 % semi-span, 27 % chord, split (upper & lower halves open for speed-brake and yaw control).
- Hardpoints: 11 (8 under wings, 3 under fuselage); wing tip rails; the wing fuel in the wing box.
- Speedbrake: split ailerons (upper half goes up, lower half goes down) opening to ~ 60°: no separate panel.

## 5. Tail

- Horizontal stabiliser: straight, span ~5.5 m (y ±2.75), root chord ~2.4 m, tip ~ 1.9 m, ~ 0° sweep, elevators ~ 40 % chord on the aft; ~0° dihedral; mounted on the tail boom at d ≈ 14.0–16.2, y ≈ +0.3.
- Vertical tails: two, at the stabiliser ends (y = ±2.75, centred on the tip), height ~ 2.6 m, root chord ~ 3.0 m, tip chord ~ 1.5 m, LE sweep ~ 15°, no cant; rudders ~ 35 % chord; tip height 4.47 m above ground.
- No ventrals.

## 6. Propulsion

- Two General Electric TF34-GE-100A high-bypass turbofans (BPR 6.2): 9,065 lbf (40.3 kN) static each; no afterburner. Total 18,130 lbf.
- Nacelles: ~ 3.9 m long, ~ 1.1 m max dia, mounted on pylons (centre x = ±1.3 m, y = +0.9 relative to the CG; stand off the fuselage ~ 0.3 m with an air gap); inlet face at d ≈ 9.0 m, nozzle at d ≈ 12.7 m (high-bypass fan nozzle slightly short of the core nozzle).
- Intake: round, plain lip, ~ 0.95 m dia, fan face; nozzles round (~ 0.9 m dia fan, ~ 0.6 core). 
- Engines are mounted high to avoid gun gas ingestion and ground debris, and so they are shielded from ground fire by the tail/wings.

- Gear positions measured from the nose tip: nose leg d = 2.65 m, main legs d = 7.75 m.
- cgHeight (body origin above ground when parked): **1.65 m**; all contact points at y = −cgHeight (level ground).

- Tricycle; nose gear single wheel offset ~ 0.3 m to the right (starboard), retracts forward, exposed partially; main gear retracts forward into the nacelles/wing roots, wheels left half-exposed; the main-gear track is ~ 5.0 m with the large single wheels.
- Static attitude: slightly nose-low (~ 2°); fuselage underside clearance ~ 0.5 m.
- No arresting hook (some versions: none); no drag chute; no catapult bar.
- Gear doors: partial main doors; nose gear door folds.

## 8. Cockpit

Pilot eye point (body axes): [0, 0.95, -3.95].

Single-seat bubble canopy (frameless, hinged at the rear, opening to the side) set high on the nose, with the pilot seated forward of the wing LE. Eye point ~ 3.4 m from the nose, ~ 0.95 m above CG, centred. HUD: yes (HUD above the instrument panel, wide FOV). The pilot sits in a titanium armoured "bathtub" ~ 1.5 m deep.

## 10. Performance

- Max level speed at sea level ~ 195 m/s (~ 380 kt, M 0.57); max Mach ~ 0.75 in a dive (limit); cruise ~ 150 m/s (~ 300 kt) at altitude (low, ~ 3,000 m ceiling in combat); service ceiling 13,636 m (44,700 ft); climb ~ 30 m/s (~ 6,000 ft/min).
- Stall ~ 55 m/s (clean, ~ 105 kt at light weight; ~ 52 m/s with flaps 20° at typical weight), approach ~ 65 m/s (~ 125 kt), rotation ~ 62 m/s; take-off roll ~ 1,200 m at max weight (~ 450 m light); landing roll ~ 600 m.
- Range ~ 4,150 km ferry; combat radius ~ 460 km with 2 h loiter. 
- Turn: sustained ~ 12 °/s (low altitude), instantaneous ~ 18 °/s; roll rate ~ 120–160 °/s. G limits +7.33 / −3.0 (clean). 
- T/W ~ 0.4 at typical (dry; no AB); wing loading ~ 330 kg/m²; glide ratio ~ 13:1 (est.).

## 11. Flight controls and special systems

- Conventional (cable and pulley, hydraulic power), with an emergency manual reversion mode (a rugged design to fly even with major damage). 
- Surfaces: elevator ×2 (no all-moving), split ailerons (with speedbrake function), twin rudders (the rudder pair also has a trim), slotted flaps, a pitch-trim stabiliser incidence.
- Flaps: UP / 7° / 10° / 20° (maneuver ~ 10°) / 28° (landing); speed brake: split ailerons open ~ 60°. Gear: up/down; landing-gear down ~ 7 s.
- No FBW; stall onset is gentle and the plane is forgiving.

## 12. Markings and finish

- Finish: U.S. Air Force overall "Compass Ghost" grey: ~ #8d9298 upper (FS 36118/36170 ~ #6f757a Dark Ghost grey) with lighter underside ~ #9ea3a8; at one time "European One" green/dark-green camo (~ #3a4a33 / #4a5a46 / #1f2a1e).
- National insignia: low-vis dark grey (~ #50555a) star-and-bar on the fuselage (aft of the cockpit) and upper left wing; tail code (e.g. "AF" etc.) on the outer fins in black/grey with fictional squadron stripes; serial on the vertical fin; "U.S. AIR FORCE" in black on the aft fuselage.
- Gun muzzle: black/grey scorching on the nose; nozzle area dark ~ #4a4a4a; the nose radome tip ~ black.
